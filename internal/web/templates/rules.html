How borrowing works

Borrowing

You can have up to {{$p.MaxLoans}} {{if eq $p.MaxLoans 1}}item{{else}}items{{end}} out at a time, each for {{$p.LoanDays}} days. Bring your library card to the desk.

{{if .Data.ClosedDays}}

We're closed on {{range $i, $d := .Data.ClosedDays}}{{if $i}} and {{end}}{{$d}}{{end}}, so nothing is ever due back then.

{{end}} {{with .Data.ClosedDates}}

We're also closed on {{range $i, $d := .}}{{if $i}}, {{end}}{{$d.Format "2 January"}}{{end}}.

{{end}}

Renewing

{{if $p.MaxRenewals}}

You can renew each loan {{if eq $p.MaxRenewals 1}}once{{else}}up to {{$p.MaxRenewals}} times{{end}} from your account, unless it's overdue or someone else is waiting for it.

{{else}}

Loans can't be renewed. Return the book and borrow it again if nobody is waiting.

{{end}}

Holds

{{if $p.MaxHolds}}

When every copy of a book is out you can place a hold from its page and join the queue. The first member in the queue gets the next copy that comes back; we keep it at the desk for {{$p.HoldPickupDays}} days. You can have up to {{$p.MaxHolds}} holds at once.

{{else}}

We don't take holds at the moment.

{{end}}

Late returns

{{if $p.FinePerDay}}

A late item costs {{money $p.FinePerDay $p.Currency}} a day{{if $p.FineCap}}, never more than {{money $p.FineCap $p.Currency}} per item{{end}}.{{with $p.FineGraceDays}} Items up to {{.}} {{if eq . 1}}day{{else}}days{{end}} late cost nothing.{{end}}

{{if $p.BlockAt}}

If you owe {{money $p.BlockAt $p.Currency}} or more you can't borrow until you've paid at the desk.

{{end}} {{else}}

We don't charge for late returns, but please bring things back so others can read them.

{{end}}
{{end}}
